# Bellynote

**Track what you eat. Understand how you feel.**

Bellynote is a food diary and symptom tracker that helps people connect what's on their plate to how their gut responds. Snap a photo, we find the pattern. No spreadsheet, no guilt.

## What it does

- **AI meal scanner.** Snap a photo and Bellynote picks out the ingredients. Prefer another way in? Scan a barcode, snap a nutrition label, or search the food database.
- **Symptom logging.** A simple 0 to 10 severity scale, right alongside meals and activities.
- **Trigger analysis.** Every ingredient is sorted into one of 12 food groups, so patterns show up even when the meals look nothing alike. Heatmaps and timelines show what lines up with rough days.
- **Cycle tracking.** See cravings and flare-ups in context, next to meals and symptoms.
- **Sage.** An AI assistant you can ask in plain language, with answers pulled from your own logs.
- **One timeline.** Meals, symptoms, activity, and cycle days on a single daily view and calendar.

## Who it's for

People managing IBS, SIBO, Crohn's, a FODMAP or gluten-free diet, or an elimination diet, and anyone who's just curious why their stomach acts up. No food is "bad" here. Bellynote notices patterns, it doesn't hand out guilt.

## A note on health

Bellynote is built to help you and your care team see the full picture. It isn't a substitute for medical advice, so always talk to a doctor about symptoms that concern you.
